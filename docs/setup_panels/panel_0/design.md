As seen in [the image](design.png), we want a pattern made of rhombuses; the middle 4 (in the pattern seen in the image) should be colored with their respective colors, except for the green tiles. Green indicates that the color of the line should be the one of the currently selected tile, and a mix of them in between (example, say we want to determine the color of the first green line, we've got a couple cases: a. cursor's hoevering/tab is selecting "Hardcoded": red; b. cursor's hovering/tab is selecting "Automated": pink; c. neither is selected/the cursor is hovering none: the mix between the specific red and pink).
